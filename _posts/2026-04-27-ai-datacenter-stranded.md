The most advanced AI infrastructure in the world is already outdated.

Not eventually. Right now.

Nvidia ships a new GPU architecture every two years. And a new chip every one.

- Hopper in 2022.
- Blackwell in 2024.
- Rubin in 2026.

Each generation doesn't just get faster. It gets physically incompatible with what came before.

- The H100 needs 700 watts per GPU.
- The B200 needs 1,000.
- A full Blackwell rack draws 120 kilowatts.

Pure air cooling maxes out at 20 kilowatts.

Blackwell racks need 6x that.

The datacenters built for H100 worked perfectly. They're just stranded now. You can't patch a building.

Hyperscalers aren't upgrading. They're replacing.

Terrifying if you're the one writing the infrastructure budget.
