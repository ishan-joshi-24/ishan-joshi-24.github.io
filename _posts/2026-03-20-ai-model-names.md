Ever wondered why AI models are named what they are?

**Claude**, named after Claude Shannon, the father of information theory. He invented the concept of a "bit." Without him, no digital communication, no internet, no AI.

**GPT**, Generative Pre-trained Transformer. No story, no personality. Just a technical description.

**Gemini**, Google named it this because it was born from merging two rival internal teams: DeepMind and Google Brain. Gemini means twins.

**LLaMA**, Large Language Model Meta AI. A backronym forced into a cute animal name.

**Grok**, from Robert Heinlein's sci-fi novel *Stranger in a Strange Land*. It means to understand something so deeply it becomes part of you.

**Copilot**, not the pilot, the assistant. Microsoft deliberately positioned AI as the sidekick, not the replacement. Smart naming for adoption.

**Perplexity**, named after the metric used to evaluate language models. Lower perplexity means better predictions. They named their company after an evaluation score.

The names tell you a lot about the company behind them.

Some want to sound smart. Some want to sound friendly. Some just picked a cute animal.
